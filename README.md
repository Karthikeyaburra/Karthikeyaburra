## 🧠 About Me

I'm a CS undergrad at SRM University Amaravati building at the intersection of **ML research and real-world engineering systems**. I go from reading papers to shipping production deployments.

- 🔬 Currently implementing an **F1 race strategy engine** from a research paper — LSTM-based tyre degradation & pit stop prediction, full-stack deployed
- 🤖 Building a **C++ ML library from scratch** — autograd, backprop, and neural net layers, no frameworks
- 🛤️ **Indian Railways collaborator** — engineered IoT track geometry measurement system with South Central Railway
- 🏆 **5th nationally** at HACK MSC 2.0 (Microsoft Student Community) · **Top 50/422** at Smart India Hackathon 2025
- 📖 Deep Learning specialization (Andrew Ng) — reproducing research papers end-to-end
- 🔧 Member, **Next Tech Lab** — robotics, IoT, intelligent hardware
